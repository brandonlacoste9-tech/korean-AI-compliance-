# Market Analysis: Korean AI Compliance

## The Opportunity

**98% of Korean AI startups are unprepared** for the AI Basic Act. Only 2% have any compliance framework in place.

**Penalties:** Up to KRW 30 million ($20,400 USD)  
**Deadline:** Grace period ending ~2027  
**Market:** Thousands of SMBs scrambling to comply

---

## Your Target Segments

### 1. Korean AI Startups (2-20 people)
**Pain:** "No bandwidth to translate policy into operational systems"  
**Solution:** Lightweight, API-first compliance that doesn't slow deployment  
**Price:** ₩99,000/month ($70) vs Enterprise custom pricing

### 2. Foreign SaaS Entering Korea
**Pain:** Must appoint domestic representative; unclear "high-impact" classification  
**Solution:** "Korea Compliance-in-a-Box" — assessment + local rep + docs  
**Price:** Bundle with compliance tool

### 3. API-First AI Products
**Pain:** Need continuous monitoring, not quarterly audits  
**Solution:** Developer-first compliance with real-time alerts, CI/CD hooks

### 4. GenAI App Builders  
**Pain:** Must label AI content, provide "meaningful explanations"  
**Solution:** Drop-in SDK (React/React Native) for labeling + notifications

### 5. Korean Legal Teams
**Pain:** Confusion about scope, "gray zone of interpretation"  
**Solution:** AI-powered documentation generator — draft assessments, model cards, reports
